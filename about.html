About Us

We are a team dedicated to providing high-quality coffee in Belfast.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver a non-corporate alternative to the chains which dominate the city. Belfast Bean Co. provides real homemade coffee that tastes like home.

Our Story

Belfast Bean Co. was founded in 2026 based on the lifelong dream of our founders. We plan to share our homemade coffee with our community for years to come.
